I am a dentist and CBCT researcher exploring the intersection of **clinical dentistry, 3D medical imaging, and applied data analysis**. I completed my Master's thesis on CBCT upper-airway analysis at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City.

Driven by curiosity, I build exploratory tools and workflows for dental imaging and education. My long-term goal is to pursue PhD research in medical imaging, health informatics, or dental AI.

### 🔬 Research Focus & Interests
* **3D Medical Imaging & Quantitative Research**: CBCT/CT image processing, airway morphometrics, crown-root ratios, CEJ mapping, and craniofacial analysis.
* **Dental AI & Educational Software**: Experimental work with multimodal LLMs, TotalSegmentator, and AI-assisted radiography feedback; these are not clinically validated systems.
* **Exploratory Healthcare Data Science**: CTGAN/ctdGAN experiments with mock tabular data for learning and distributional evaluation, not a privacy guarantee for clinical data.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Programming & Analysis**: Python, R, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Jupyter
* **Data Querying**: Basic SQL
* **Medical Imaging**: CBCT morphometrics, NiBabel, exploratory TotalSegmentator use, ITK-SNAP
* **AI & Software Projects**: Gemini API and prompt design, CTGAN, ctdGAN, Git/GitHub, and AI-assisted TypeScript/React/Node development
