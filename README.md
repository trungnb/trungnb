I am a dentist and PhD applicant researching CBCT-based oral radiology, 3D dental imaging, and quantitative analysis. I completed an MSc thesis on CBCT upper-airway analysis at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City. My published work addresses upper-airway dimensions and maxillary premolar alveolar-bone morphology. Clinical experience in oral surgery and implantology informs my research in dental imaging and exploratory educational tools.

### 🔬 Research Focus & Interests
* **CBCT and 3D imaging:** airway morphometrics, dental morphology, and craniofacial analysis.
* **Quantitative research:** reproducible analysis and visualisation of CBCT-derived measurements.
* **Exploratory AI:** educational radiograph feedback, medical-image segmentation, and synthetic tabular data.

### 🛠️ Skills & Tools
* **Data analysis (working level):** R (`dplyr`, `ggplot2`; Wilcoxon, Fisher, Cliff's delta, ROC, ICC) and Python (`pandas`, `scikit-learn`); basic SQL.
* **Medical imaging:** CBCT airway analysis in Romexis Viewer, OnDemand3D, and DTX Studio; 3D Slicer.
* **Experimenting with:** TotalSegmentator and CTGAN/ctdGAN.
* **AI-assisted work:** Google Gemini API and PeriApicaI (AI-assisted development).
