I am a clinical dentist and researcher with a Master's degree in Odonto-Stomatology from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City. My passion lies at the intersection of **Clinical Dentistry, Medical Imaging, and Artificial Intelligence**. 

Currently, I am actively building open-source tools for dental AI and preparing for Ph.D. programs in Medical/Dental AI.

### 🔬 Research Focus & Interests
* **3D Medical Imaging**: CBCT/CT scan segmentation, airway morphometrics, and craniofacial analysis.
* **Dental Artificial Intelligence**: Applying Large Language Models (LLMs) and Deep Learning (TotalSegmentator, GANs) to clinical diagnostics.
* **Data Synthesis in Healthcare**: Privacy-preserving tabular data synthesis (CTGAN/CTDGAN) for medical research.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Languages**: Python, R
* **AI/ML**: PyTorch, Generative AI (Gemini APIs), CTGAN, Scikit-Learn
* **Medical Imaging**: PyMuPDF, NiBabel, TotalSegmentator, ITK-SNAP
* **Data Science**: Pandas, NumPy, Jupyter, SQLite

### 🚀 Highlighted Projects
1. **[PeriApicaI](https://periapical.ai.studio)**: An AI-powered dental periapical radiography training platform utilizing the Gemini AI model to analyze technical errors in X-ray films.
2. **3D Craniofacial Shape Analysis**: A high-throughput deep learning pipeline for automated segmentation of craniofacial structures and teeth from NIfTI files.
3. **Medical Tabular Data Synthesis**: A privacy-preserving pipeline leveraging CTGAN & CTDGAN to generate synthetic demographic data for dental research.
4. **CEJ Morphometrics**: Automated quantitative analysis workflow for calculating Crown-Root ratios from 3D dental models.
